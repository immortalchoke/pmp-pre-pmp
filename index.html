Campaign Setup

Set up your campaign for success. Follow our recommendations below to make sure you’re showing your ads in the right places to the right people.

Objective

Promote your business locally using the Reach Objective.

Budget

Spend a minimum of $7 per week on this type of ad. Increasing your budget higher than $7 per week will improve results so you can reach more people at a better price.

Targeting

Keep your audience as broad as possible, unless you have a strong sense of age, gender, or interests of your target audience.

  • Broad targeting: Targeting broadly means that you're allowing Facebook to find the best people to show your ad to. This can lead you to find customers if you're not sure who you want to target. Tip: Try using a radius around your store location to find potential local customers.

  • Detailed targeting: This allows you to select the specific targeting you’d like to use for your ad. Detailed targeting includes demographic information, such as age, gender, location and interests. Note, while this leads to smaller audiences, it may also lead to one that's more interested in what you're advertising about.

Placement

Select Automatic Placements to get the most out of your budget. Facebook's delivery system will allocate your ad set's budget across multiple placements that'll perform best.

Tips & Tricks

Put your best strategy and creative forward. Try these helpful suggestions on how to make your ad stand out to your audience.

Strategic Solutions

  • Showcase your physical business (store, restaurant, salon, etc).

  • Play with special moments or occasions to drive people to your business.

  • Include your business's location or address so your audience knows where to visit you.

  • Add an in-person promotion code for store visiting (e.g., "First 50 customers who come in and whisper "sweet tooth" will receive a free cupcake!"

  • If you have both online and offline services give customers a reason to come to your store, such as seasonal products, markdown sales or exclusive services.

Creative Best Practices

Photo

  • Catch the viewer's eye with a strong focal point in your image.

  • Stop people as they scroll by using bright, contrasting colors and bold backgrounds to stop people as they scroll.

  • Showcase your product in action and/or highlight a customer using your product/service.

Video

  • Include your brand (logos, colors) within the first 3 seconds.

  • Highlight your product and service for the majority of the video.

  • Monitor your page for content that resonates well with your audience and shows high engagement metrics. Utilize page content that has done well in the past for future ads.

Optimization

Identify opportunities to optimize your ads by measuring performance and troubleshooting any issues.

Measure Performance

  • To access reporting, visit Ads Center from your Page (Page > Ads Center on top banner > select “All Ads” from the left-hand banner).

  • View your most recent ad promotion.

  • The metric you want to view is Reach (number of people reached throughout the duration of your ad).

  • A minimum of 50 people reached for a one-week campaign is a good baseline.

  • If you're advertising on other channels, compare how many people you reached and your cost.

Troubleshooting

  • You're not reaching 50 people per week.

  • This issue likely means your budget is too small for this objective. We recommend increasing your budget to the recommended minimum of $7 per week or higher to reach 50 people in a one-week campaign.
