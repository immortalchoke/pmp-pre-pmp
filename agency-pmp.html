Value Prop

Reach more of the people who are likely to be interested in and remember your ads. On Facebook and Instagram, where communities of people watch videos and discover new things every day.

Pitch

Using the Brand Awareness objective for Sales, like launching a new product, is a great way to not only reach a new prospective customer broad audience but it also reaches people most likely to recall your ad. Once someone can recall your ad, there is a better chance they will recall your brand when they are ready to purchase.

  • 1. Explain the benefits of the Brand Awareness objective:
  • Not only can you reach a broad audience of potential interested people but your ad will deliver to those most likely to recall/remember your brand.
  • 2. Review best practices for the campaign:
  • Choose the right audience (keep it broad and utilize our interest and demographic targeting).
  • Create a great experience for potential customers (strong, relevant creative and copy).
  • Optimize for results (Ad Recall).
  • Opt into all available placements.

Objection Handling

When should you use reach and frequency buying?

If you have been running direct response (DR) campaigns and are now looking to increase brand awareness and reach for new product launches, a major event promotion, or to drive website traffic OR If you are ready to invest in brand building campaigns and then use pixel-optimized campaigns to retarget users who saw your initial brand messaging to drive incremental conversions.

Why use reach and frequency buying vs auction buying?

Reach and frequency buying allows you to drive targeted awareness with predictable reach at controlled frequency and the ability to lock-down inventory ahead of time.

What bid types can I use with reach and frequency buying?

Reach and frequency automatically uses fixed CPM bidding. It will not work with CPC, oCPM, or CPA. The CPM is set at the time of the campaign booking.

How does reach and frequency optimization compare to the auction when I select a certain objective, say video views?

Reach and frequency uses a hybrid optimization approximately halfway between your objective and cheap reach. This means that, compared to the auction, reach and frequency should achieve a lower cost per impression and a higher cost per outcome (views, in the case of video views). This is taken into account in the prediction you see for reach and frequency.

How does reach and frequency optimization compare to the auction when I select a certain objective, say video views?

Reach and frequency uses a hybrid optimization approximately halfway between your objective and cheap reach. This means that, compared to the auction, reach and frequency should achieve a lower cost per impression and a higher cost per outcome (views, in the case of video views). This is taken into account in the prediction you see for reach and frequency.

How predictable is the reach with reach and frequency?

While reach is not guaranteed with reach and frequency, over 95% of the time we deliver within 5% of the client's reach goal. And reach matters. High reaching campaigns drive offline sales more cost-efficiently than low reaching campaigns. Reach has been, and will continue to be, an important driver of campaign effectiveness. For brand marketers, changing attitudes and behaviors at scale will ultimately lead to growing more business.
